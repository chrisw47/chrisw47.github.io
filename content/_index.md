Hi, I'm Chris. I'm a post-baccalaureate researcher working on biophysics and theoretical aspects of reservoir computing under Professor Sarah Marzen at the Claremont Colleges.

I graduated from Pomona College in May 2025 with a double major in Physics and Mathematics, and I'm now hoping to steer my research to lie at the intersection of neuroscience, physics/math, and artificial intelligence. I love seeing the robustness of interdisciplinary research, in particular how connecting techniques from one field to solve problems in another drives many discoveries and applications (examples include the MRI or artificial intelligence).

In the last few years, I've come to realize that building things, documenting the process, and writing up larger bodies of work are rewarding pursuits. In essence, working on projects! As such, I've been trying to get into that habit more, and some of the stuff I think about in time not spent doing research can be found in the [Projects](/projects/) and [Scribbles](/scribbles/) sections.
