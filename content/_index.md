Hi, I'm Chris. I'm a post-baccalaureate researcher working on biophysics and theoretical aspects of reservoir computing under Professor Sarah Marzen at the Claremont Colleges.

I graduated from Pomona College in May 2025 with a double major in Physics and Mathematics, and I'm now hoping to steer my research to lie at the intersection of neuroscience, physics/math, and artificial intelligence. I love seeing the robustness of interdisciplinary research, in particular how connecting techniques from one field to solve problems in another drives many recent groundbreaking discoveries and applications, such as the MRI, diffusion models (yes, this is biased towards the physical sciences, as I am a physicist/mathematician).

Building things and documenting the process and writing up larger bodies of work are rewarding pursuits. I've been trying to get into that habit more, and relevant work can be found in the [Projects](/projects/) and [Scribbles](/scribbles/) sections.
