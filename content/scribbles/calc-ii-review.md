Partly out of boredom, I wrote a list of review questions that one can try out after completing Calc II (or AP Calculus BC), where I take an application-centric method to motivate the importance of certain concepts. My favorite question in the set is the last one, where, in a number of steps, the problem-solver derives higher-order corrections to the standard pendulum period equation.
