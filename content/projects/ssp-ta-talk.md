I gave this talk to students while serving as a TA/RM for Summer Science Program Astro 2025 at NMSU, spanning 90 minutes and supplemented with live activities to engage students with the new concepts. It motivates the Lagrangian picture of mechanics as an alternative to Newton's forces, one built instead on energy and conservation laws, and walks through the calculus of variations to derive the Euler-Lagrange equations from the principle of least action.
