We're taught early on that the shortest path between two points is a straight line, but this only holds in flat, Euclidean space. This document develops the calculus of variations and the Euler-Lagrange equations as a general tool for finding shortest paths (geodesics) in an arbitrary space, then applies the result in spherical coordinates to show that the geodesics on a sphere are great circles.
