This is my senior thesis in Mathematics at Pomona College, advised by Professor Edray Goins. Motivated by a gap identified by the American Institute of Physics TEAM-UP task force, the thesis develops a set of multimodal (written and animated) mathematical supplement modules meant to complement the first-year physics sequence. Three of these modules were piloted during the quantum mechanics unit of Physics 70, and the thesis analyzes student responses to evaluate how well each component supported learning, closing with recommendations for extending the supplements across the introductory physics curriculum.
