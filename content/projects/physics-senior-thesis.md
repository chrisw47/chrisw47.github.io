This is my senior thesis in Physics and Astronomy at Pomona College, advised by Professor Sarah Marzen, with additional guidance from Professors Jorge Moreno and Thomas Moore. The thesis develops a Maximum Entropy model, built on principles from statistical mechanics and information theory, to infer correlations between neurons and characterize how cultured cortical neurons store memory of their environment and predict its future. The model performed well at short timescales but showed limited predictive capability further out. This same modeling approach became the basis for the [bias-variance and confusion-matrix framework](/research/maxent-sensor-bias-variance/) I later developed with Professor Marzen's lab.
